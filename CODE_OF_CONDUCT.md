# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We are committed to a welcoming and harassment-free community.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Accepting feedback gracefully
- Prioritizing collaboration and clarity

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Publishing private information without consent

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may remove comments, commits, issues, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
